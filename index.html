About SMATI

Discover our history, mission, and values

Our Story

SMATI started business January 8, 2016. Founded as the premiere institution in the city of Malabon, SMATI is a non-profit organization dedicated in identifying, preserving, and promoting public awareness in Science and Technology. Bustling to be globally competitive in Education, The Founding of the Institute is marked by remarkable vision and abiding faith of the founders in the decisive role of education and technology in raising the quality of life of the people.

We are an independent Training and Assessment Institution offering full Technical trainings and assessments for Hospitality and Tourism Management Programs for Future Restaurateurs, Hoteliers, and Tourism Professionals.

We are recognized by the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), Department of Education (DepEd) and other Government Agencies whom are part of our existence and daily operations.

Our Mission

To provide quality vocational education and training in accordance to the needs of the labour market, to contribute in the rising level of youth employment, and to improve the efficiency and the competitiveness in the economy.

Programs are enhanced by developing and maintaining partnership with industry-based business and the economy.

Our Vision

SMATI envision becoming one of the industry based educational institutions that give its students the opportunity to excel and advance their skills and knowledge with passion to be prepared in their chosen field of career.

We aim to continue the nation’s development by providing highly skilled workforce who will become assets to the society.
